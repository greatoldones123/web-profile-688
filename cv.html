• Developed a state-aware AR wayfinding system on Meta Quest 3, integrating visual positioning, route tracking, and real-time navigation-state detection to adapt when and how guidance is presented.

• Contributed to constructing the State-Aware Egocentric Navigation (SAEN) dataset with 44 participants, integrating egocentric RGB video, 6-DoF head pose, and continuous wayfinding-state annotations for cross-user state detection.
